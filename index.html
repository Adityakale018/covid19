About COVID-19

The essentials everyone should know.

What it is

COVID-19 is an infectious respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 coronavirus. It was first identified in late 2019.

How it spreads

The virus spreads mainly through tiny respiratory droplets and aerosols when people are close together, especially indoors.

Incubation period

Symptoms usually appear between 2 and 14 days after exposure. Some people spread the virus without feeling ill.

Who is most at risk

Older adults and people with heart disease, diabetes, chronic lung disease, or weakened immune systems face higher risk of severe illness.

Know the signs

COVID-19 symptoms

Symptoms range from mild to severe and can look like a cold or flu. If you notice several signs, consider testing and staying home.

Common symptoms

  • Fever or chills
  • Cough
  • Shortness of breath or difficulty breathing
  • Fatigue and muscle or body aches
  • Headache
  • Loss of taste or smell
  • Sore throat and congestion
  • Nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea

Emergency warning signs

Get medical help right away if you or someone with you has:

  • Trouble breathing or breathlessness at rest
  • Persistent pain or pressure in the chest
  • New confusion or difficulty staying awake
  • Bluish lips or face

Call your local emergency number. Do not wait for test results.

What to do if you feel unwell

  1. Stay home and rest, away from others where you can.
  2. Take a COVID-19 test as soon as possible.
  3. Contact a healthcare provider, especially if you are at higher risk.
  4. Wear a well-fitting mask around others and wash your hands often.
Stay protected

Prevention guidelines

Simple everyday habits reduce the chance of catching and spreading COVID-19, especially when combined.

v

Get vaccinated

Stay up to date with recommended vaccines and boosters to lower your risk of severe illness.

~

Improve ventilation

Open windows and use air filters indoors. Fresh air quickly dilutes airborne virus.

m

Wear a mask

Use a well-fitting mask in crowded or poorly ventilated spaces and around people who are unwell.

w

Wash your hands

Clean your hands with soap and water for at least 20 seconds, or use an alcohol-based rub.

c

Cover your cough

Cough or sneeze into a tissue or your elbow, then wash your hands.

h

Stay home when sick

Keep your distance from others while you have symptoms, and test if you are unsure.

Protect people at higher risk

Before visiting older relatives or friends with health conditions, consider testing, wearing a mask, and meeting in well-ventilated spaces.
